Voice report · Wolves of Blood Moon · episodes 11–20

What WOBM listeners say about the voices, eps 11–20

Agents read all 150 WOBM comments on eps 11–20 and tagged every one that mentions voice, narration or how characters sound. The 186 TAB comments on the same episodes went through the same tagging as a benchmark.

17%of WOBM comments are about voice (25 of 150). TAB: 27%
23of those 25 are negative; the other 2 are mixed. None praise a voice.
29% → 17%share of WOBM comments about voice, eps 1–10 vs eps 11–20
3comments say the voice changed, down from 30 in eps 1–10. TAB: 14

What this says

  1. By ep 11, voice stops being WOBM's outlier. 17% of eps 11–20 comments mention voice, below TAB's 27% on the same episodes. In eps 1–10 WOBM ran at 29% against TAB's 10%.
  2. The "voice changed" complaint is an entry-point problem. Ad-mismatch comments fall from 23 in eps 1–10 to 3 here, all in eps 11–12. Nobody in eps 11–20 says the voice changed from an earlier version or between episodes.
  3. What's left is the narrator herself. 15 of the 25 comments call the voices bad ("I hate this narrator", "this chick is terrible at narrating"), and 7 still object to a woman voicing the men.
  4. Some who stay complain and keep listening. 4 of the 25 voice comments also praise the story ("Loving the story… An issue that could improve is having a male narrators for the men"). Only 1 threatens to quit, against 7 in eps 1–10.
  5. Eps 11–12 carry the tail. 18 of the 25 voice comments sit in eps 11–12; eps 13–19 have 3 of 89. Recasting the men's voices in eps 1–12 covers almost every complaint in this report too.

How I built this

  • Source: in-app comment exports. WOBM comments run 1 Aug – 8 Oct 2026, TAB 29 Aug – 8 Oct 2026. All comments on eps 11–20: WOBM 150, TAB 186. Eps 1–10 figures come from the same tagging run.
  • One Sonnet agent per show read every comment and tagged voice mentions with a fixed schema (complaint types, characters, quit intent). Several tags per comment are allowed, so the type counts add up to more than the total.
  • Single episodes here hold 5–26 comments and WOBM has only 25 voice comments in total, so per-episode shares swing hard. Read the shape, not single bars.
  • Comments tell you what listeners say, not who leaves. Pair this with the retention export to see whether the quiet in eps 13–19 means listeners adapted or left.
  • Main block report: index.html. Eps 1–20 voice report: voice.html.
