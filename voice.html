Voice report · Wolves of Blood Moon · episodes 1–20

What WOBM listeners say about the voices, eps 1–20

Agents read all 490 WOBM comments on eps 1–20 and tagged every one that mentions voice, narration or how characters sound. The 326 TAB comments on the same episodes went through the same tagging as a benchmark.

26%of WOBM comments are about voice (125 of 490). TAB: 20%
121of those 125 are negative. 1 praises a voice.
45%of all likes on eps 1–20 go to voice comments (43 of 96). Other listeners agree.
33comments say the voice changed: from the ad, an earlier version, or mid-show

What to do

  1. Cast male voices for the male leads in eps 1–12. 38 comments object to one woman voicing Asher and the alphas, and 20 ask outright for male voices. Eps 1–12 hold 118 of the 125 voice comments.
  2. Make the ad sound like ep 1, or ep 1 sound like the ad. The ad plays as a dramatized scene with male and female voices. The app opens as one narrator telling the story. 26 comments call this out, against 5 on TAB.
  3. Give listeners a voice setting. 8 comments ask how to switch the narration off, mute it or pick another speaker.
  4. Check retention at eps 1–12. Voice complaints fall from 31% of comments in eps 1–12 to 3% in eps 13–19. Either listeners get used to the voice, or the ones who mind it have left. The retention curve will show which.

How I built this

  • Source: in-app comment exports. WOBM comments run 1 Aug – 8 Oct 2026, TAB 29 Aug – 8 Oct 2026. All comments on eps 1–20: WOBM 490, TAB 326.
  • One Sonnet agent per show read every comment and tagged voice mentions with a fixed schema (complaint types, characters, quit intent). Several tags per comment are allowed, so the type counts add up to more than the total.
  • Single episodes hold 5–80 comments, so per-episode shares swing hard. Read the shape, not single bars.
  • Comments tell you what listeners say, not who leaves. Pair this with the eps 1–20 retention export before acting on the ep 13–19 drop.
  • Main block report: index.html.
